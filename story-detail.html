KL Conference on a New Just & Humane International Order

Policymakers, scholars and civil society examined the failures of the current international system from Global South perspectives.

14 October 2025

Photography
Date
14 October 2025
Location
Kuala Lumpur

On 14 October 2025, Suluh Centre convened policymakers, scholars and civil society in Kuala Lumpur to examine where the current international system is failing, and what a more just and humane order could look like from Global South perspectives.

The day's sessions traced a throughline from the Siyasah Unit's original research agenda to a wider network of institutions now carrying the same questions forward. In the live site, this is the full write-up, entered by the KL team in the CMS.
