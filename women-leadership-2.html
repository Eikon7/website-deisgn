Wanita Mendunia 2.0

The advanced track for Wanita Mendunia graduates who are ready to mentor the next cohort and step into formal roles in local governance.

What it is

The path beyond the first cohort

Wanita Mendunia 2.0 is not a repeat of the original training — it is what comes after it. Graduates who have already led in their own communities take on a second role: mentoring the next cohort coming up behind them.

Why it exists

Leadership that keeps building on itself

A single cohort of trained leaders is a good outcome. A chain of mentorship, where each cohort trains the next, is a durable one. 2.0 exists so the programme's impact does not reset with every new intake.

How it works

Mentoring, formal roles, and a wider reach

Selected graduates co-facilitate the next cohort's training sessions alongside Suluh Centre's team, and are supported to put themselves forward for formal roles — village development committees, local councils, and community organisations — where the leadership they have already practised informally can carry more weight.
