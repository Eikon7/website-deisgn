Community · Care & Wellbeing

Projek Komuniti Permata

Care work holds communities together and is rarely counted as work at all. Projek Komuniti Permata makes it visible, brings support directly to where people already are, and treats wellbeing as shared community infrastructure.

Community-based care initiative
What it is

Care, brought into the open

Projek Komuniti Permata organises the caregiving that already happens in every community — looking after elderly parents, watching neighbours' children, checking in on someone struggling — into a supported, visible network, with regular health and mental health screening days brought directly to where people live.

Why it exists

The care economy holds up everything and shows up nowhere

Caregiving is unpaid, undercounted, and falls disproportionately on women. It does not appear in economic statistics, but nothing else in a community functions without it. Projek Komuniti Permata exists to give that work recognition, structure and practical support.

How it works

Screening days and a local care network

Free health and mental health screening days are run in partnership with local clinics, bringing basic checks to people who would otherwise have to travel for them. Alongside this, a network of community caregivers is trained and connected, so support does not depend on any one person carrying the load alone.
