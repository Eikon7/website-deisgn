About

Building communities with purpose

Suluh Centre is the natural evolution of Polity — the same people and the same grassroots strength, growing into what the work was always becoming. We are independent, and we work across Malaysia.

We don't believe change is handed down. We believe it is grown, patiently, from the ground up.

Vision

A Malaysia where a just and humane society can see its way forward: where communities, young people, and honest ideas light the path, and no one is left in the dark.

Mission

Suluh exists to carry light where it is needed most. We illuminate public life with ideas grounded in evidence and values. We empower communities to lead their own change. We equip the next generation, and the wider public, to see and shape the future.
