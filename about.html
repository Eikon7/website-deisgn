About

Who we are

An institution built from the ground up

Suluh Centre is an independent, secular, national Malaysian civic institution working across community leadership, youth and education, and ideas. We start where change actually grows — in communities, in young people, and in the values we build on.

Suluh Centre
Continuity

From Polity to Suluh

Polity always believed in change from the ground up. Suluh is that belief carried all the way through. Nothing was dropped.

2024

Polity is founded

An independent Malaysian centre, built from the ground up on community leadership, research and evidence-led advocacy.

2026

Suluh Centre carries it forward

The same team, the same grassroots strength, a sharper reason for being. The name is new. The work is not.

What that name means

Independent · Secular · National

Independent
No political party or single funder shapes our direction.
Secular
Open to people of every faith and none, working from shared values.
National
Rooted in Malaysia, working across states and communities.
What we stand for

Four things we don't compromise on

01

Leadership that's already there

We don't parachute in expertise. We recognise and back the leadership communities already practise.

02

Evidence over ideology

Research and lived experience decide our positions, not a fixed political line.

03

Women and youth at the centre

Not a diversity add-on — central to how every programme is designed.

04

Change that stays local

The people affected by a decision should be the ones shaping it.
